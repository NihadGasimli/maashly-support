Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Maashly app processes, why it is used and how it is protected. The app is operated by Azsoftware LLC (55/1 Rashid Behbudov Street, AF City Business Center, 16th floor, Baku, Azerbaijan).

1. What Maashly is

Maashly is a mobile app that lets employees of private companies connected to Maashly view their own salary information. Salary data is shown directly from your employer's system.

2. What data we process

Identity data. When you sign in with SIMA, your FIN (personal identification number), first name and last name are received from SIMA. This data is used to check whether you are an employee of your employer.

Salary data. Net salary, earnings and deductions (category, description, amount) are retrieved from your employer's system over an encrypted connection only to be shown to you. Maashly does not store this data on its servers and does not view it.

Logs and technical data. Maashly has a zero-log policy. User activity, requests, IP addresses, device information and error logs are not collected or stored. The app contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.

Settings. Settings you choose in the app (e.g. hiding the amount) are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.

We do not access payment cards, bank accounts, location, contacts, photos or the microphone.

3. How we use data

  • To securely identify you through SIMA
  • To verify that you are an employee of your employer
  • To show you your own salary information
  • To keep the app secure, stable and easy to use
  • To meet obligations required by law

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or tracking across apps and websites, and we do not sell it.

4. Who we share data with

Your data is never sold to third parties. For the service to work, data is transferred only in the following cases:

  • Your employer: your FIN is sent to your employer's system to verify your employment status and retrieve your salary data. Your employer is the owner and source of your salary data.
  • SIMA: identification is performed through the SIMA electronic signature system.
  • Legal requirements: when there is a lawful request from competent government authorities.

5. Security

  • Sign-in is possible only with a SIMA electronic signature.
  • All traffic between the app and servers is encrypted with SSL/TLS.
  • Each company's data is kept isolated from other companies.

6. Data retention

Session data is deleted when you sign out or when the token expires. Settings on your device are deleted when the app is removed. Under our zero-log policy, activity and technical logs are not stored at all. Your employer is responsible for retaining salary data.

7. Your rights

Maashly has a zero-log policy and does not store any data about you on its servers. This means we hold no personal data to delete, correct or export. You control your data as follows:

  • Data on your device: the session is deleted when you sign out, and all settings are deleted when you remove the app.
  • Salary data: your employer owns this data. To access, correct or delete it, contact your employer's HR or accounting department.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can stop using the service at any time by signing out, removing the app and revoking the permission you granted in SIMA.

For privacy questions, write to office@azsoftware.az. More on managing your choices: Privacy choices.

8. Children

Maashly is intended for working employees and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will give notice of significant changes in the app or on this page in advance. The date at the top of the page shows the latest update.
