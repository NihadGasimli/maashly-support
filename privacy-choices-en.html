Privacy Choices

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Maashly shows no ads, does not sell your data and does not track you across other apps or websites. We have a zero-log policy: no data about you is stored on our servers. Below is how you can manage your data and send us a request.

What you can do in the app

Sign out

Tap the sign-out button on the Profile screen. The session is deleted from your device, and SIMA confirmation is required at the next sign-in.

Delete the app

Removing the app deletes all data it stored on your device (session, settings). Salary data is never stored permanently on the device.

Hide the amount

Tap the eye icon on the home screen to hide or show your salary amount.

Revoke SIMA access

You can revoke the sign-in permission granted to Maashly in the active sessions or permissions section of the SIMA app.

iOS permissions

You can change the app's device permissions at any time in Settings → Maashly.

Wrong salary data

Salary, earnings and deductions come from your employer's system. For corrections or deletion, contact your company's HR or accounting department.

Send a privacy request

To request deletion or a copy of your data, withdraw consent, or ask any privacy question, send us an email. Include your full name and the company you work for. Because we hold no stored data about you, we answer deletion and copy requests with a written confirmation of this. We respond to requests within 30 days.

Send email office@azsoftware.az

Do not include your FIN or other ID document details.
